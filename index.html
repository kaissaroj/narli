Good to know

The questions parents ask most.

How do I open the grown-ups area?

Tap For grown-ups at the top left of the home screen and answer the little question. It keeps settings, the purchase and outside links away from small fingers.

What's free, and what does "Unlock Everything" include?

Some pictures are free to color right away. One purchase of Unlock Everything opens every coloring book, plus every new book we add later. It's a one-time purchase, never a subscription.

I bought it before. How do I get it back?

Open the grown-ups area and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Restoring is always free.

Can my family use it too?

Yes! Unlock Everything works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your Apple family gets it on their own iPhone or iPad. If the books don't unlock by themselves, tap Restore purchase on that device.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find Narli and choose Request a refund.

My child's pictures disappeared.

Colored pictures are saved only on the device, for privacy. They're removed if the app is deleted or the device is reset without a backup. The purchase is never lost: just tap Restore purchase.

How do I turn off the music or sounds?

In the grown-ups area, switch Music or Sound effects off.

Does Narli need the internet?

No. Coloring works fully offline, great for car rides and flights. The internet is only needed to buy or restore Unlock Everything.

Are there ads? Do you collect my child's data?

No ads, no tracking, no accounts. The privacy page explains everything in plain words.

Still stuck?

Tell us your device (like iPhone 15 or iPad Air) and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.
