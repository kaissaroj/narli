Color them in Narli

12 coloring books in one calm app for little kids. No ads, no accounts, and a grown-ups check on every purchase.

Is it right for a 2-year-old?

Yes. Each picture has one big friend and no tiny parts, and one tap fills a whole space, so even the youngest finish a picture.

What's free?

The app is a free download with four pictures to color right away: three zoo animals and a gingerbread house. One purchase unlocks every book. It is not a subscription.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

No. Coloring works fully offline, in the car or on a plane.

Are there ads?

No ads, no tracking and no accounts. Purchases and links sit behind a grown-ups check.
