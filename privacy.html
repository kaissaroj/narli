Privacy

Your child colors. Nobody watches. Here's exactly what happens with information in Narli.

Our five promises

The short version, for busy grown-ups.

  • 🚫No ads, ever
  • 👀No tracking or analytics
  • 🔑No accounts, no sign-in, no chat
  • 🤫We never ask for your child's name, photo, voice or location
  • 🏠Paintings stay on your device

What stays with you

Saved only on the device, so Narli opens the way your child left it. We can't see any of it.

On the device

  • the pictures your child has colored
  • music and sound settings
  • whether the full version is unlocked

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Never collected

No names, emails, phone numbers, photos, drawings, voice, contacts, location or advertising IDs. Narli doesn't use the camera, microphone or photo library, and has no ads, analytics tools or social features.

Only for the purchase

One thing leaves the device: the one-time purchase that unlocks every book. Apple takes the payment, and RevenueCat (a service for in-app purchases) checks it so you can restore it later.

Purchase history

Whether "Unlock Everything" was bought, and Apple's receipt. Used to unlock the books and restore them.

Apple · RevenueCat
A random app ID

Made up by the app. Not linked to your name, email or Apple Account. Used to match the purchase to this app.

RevenueCat
Basic tech details

App version, device type, system version, country and IP address, sent with the purchase check so it works.

RevenueCat

None of this is linked to your identity, used for advertising, or used to track you or your child across other apps or websites. We never sell or rent information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Made for kids

Children's privacy

Because Narli collects no personal information from children, we never ask for a child's details. Settings, the purchase and any link that leaves the app sit behind a parental gate, a simple question for grown-ups.

Your rights

Delete the app to remove everything on the device. To ask about, or delete, purchase information RevenueCat holds for Narli, email kaissaroj@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may also have rights to access, correct or delete information and to complain to a data protection authority. We'll gladly help.

How long it's kept

Device data stays until you delete the app. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as long as needed for restores and their legal and accounting duties.

Changes

If anything changes we'll update this page and its date. If a change ever means collecting new information, we'll update the App Store privacy details first.

Effective 28 October 2026. Narli: Coloring Book for Kids is made by Saroj Subedi and follows Apple's Kids category rules, COPPA and the GDPR.
